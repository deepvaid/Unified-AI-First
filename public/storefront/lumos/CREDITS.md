# Photo credits: Lumos Home (demo) store

Every photo in this folder comes from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the
[Unsplash licence](https://unsplash.com/license) (free to use, commercial use allowed, no
attribution required). Only free-licence photos were used, never Unsplash+. They were downloaded
on 2026-11-24, cropped and resized by Unsplash's image service (products 1200 x 1321, the product
card's 248:273 shape; room tiles 1200 x 900; hero 1600 x 1000; page banner 1600 x 900) and saved
as JPEG at quality 80, or lower (down to 46) where a photo would otherwise be too large, so the
folder stays under 8 MB. They are served from this repo: the storefront never loads a picture from
another site.

Every photo was checked by eye: no brand name, logo, watermark or recognisable face is in shot.
`room-office.jpg` is cropped to leave out a bookshelf with readable spines, `mesa-table-2.jpg` to
leave out a candle label and `cloud-bedding-clay-1.jpg` to leave out a poster. Some pictures are 3D
renders by interior design studios (`hero-living.jpg`, `room-living.jpg`, `room-bedroom.jpg`,
`room-dining.jpg`, `room-outdoor.jpg`, `arlo-sofa-*`, `fold-table-*`). The two photos of
a product show the same piece: `arlo-sofa-2`, `haven-nightstand-2`, `ledger-desk-2`,
`grid-bookshelf-2`, `cove-sofa-2` and `lantern-set-2` are closer crops of the product's first
photo, `halden-console-2` is a closer crop of a second shot of the same sideboard, and
`weave-rug-1` is a closer crop of `weave-rug-2`, so the rug leads and the sofa behind it is only a corner. The colour photos show the same kind of piece in that colour:
`ergo-chair-black-1.jpg` is the same chair in black from the same shoot, while the Sage and Clay
bedding photos are other photographers' beds made up in that colour.

The six brand logos are made-up wordmarks drawn for this demo as SVG paths (no font files);
none of them is a real company's mark. To replace a photo or a logo, save a new file under the
same name.
