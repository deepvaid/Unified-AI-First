Every photo in this folder comes from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the
[Unsplash licence](https://unsplash.com/license) or from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com) under
the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (both free to use, commercial use allowed,
no attribution required). Only free-licence photos were used, never Unsplash+. Most were
downloaded on 2026-11-19, cropped and resized by Unsplash's image service (products 1200 x 1200,
category tiles 1200 x 900, stories 1600 x 1200, hero 1600 x 800). The five added on 2026-11-23
(`battery-standard-1.jpg`, `halogen-bulbs-1.jpg`, `cabin-air-filter-1.jpg`,
`brake-rotor-truck-2.jpg`, `brake-rotor-car-2.jpg`) were cropped on the part by hand to
1200 x 1200. All are re-encoded as JPEG and served from this repo: the storefront never loads a
picture from another site.

Every photo was checked by eye: no part brand, oil brand, vehicle badge or readable license plate
is in shot. Real cars appear only as anonymous bodywork, wheels, lights and engine bays. Some
product shots show a mechanic's hands. `cabin-air-filter-1.jpg` is cropped below the filter
frame's top edge, which carries the maker's print. To replace a photo, save a new file under the
same name.

## Close matches, not exact

These photos are the nearest free, unbranded match for the shot list; swap them if a better
photo turns up.

- **Brake pads (4001, 4002).** No free, unbranded photo of loose brake pads turned up on Unsplash
  or Pexels, so these photos show the pads in place, inside the caliper against the rotor.
- **`engine-air-filter-1.jpg`** shows a cone-type engine air filter, not a flat panel filter. The
  only free flat panel engine filter found was dirty and half hidden in its airbox.
- **`oil-filter-cartridge-1.jpg`** is a studio shot of a filter element; its background has a faint
  light-grey checker pattern baked in.
- **`tire-gauge-1.jpg`** shows an analogue dial gauge on a red car body; the product is a digital
  gauge. No free photo of a digital tire gauge turned up.
- **`hero.jpg`**: the pickup on the dirt road is too far away for any lettering on it to be read.
