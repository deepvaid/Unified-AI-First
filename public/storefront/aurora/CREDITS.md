# Photo credits: Aurora Outdoor (demo) store

Every photo in this folder comes from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the
[Unsplash licence](https://unsplash.com/license) (free to use, commercial use allowed, no
attribution required). Only free-licence photos were used, never Unsplash+. They were downloaded
on 2026-11-24, cropped and resized by Unsplash's image service (products 1200 x 1200, the square
photo of Aurora's product card; Featured Collections tiles 1200 x 900; hero 1600 x 800; story
1200 x 1500), then re-encoded as JPEG at quality 82, or lower (down to 46) where a photo would
otherwise be too large, so the folder stays under 8 MB. They are served from this repo: the
storefront never loads a picture from another site.

Every photo was checked by eye: no brand name, logo, watermark or readable text is in shot. Outdoor
gear often carries its maker's mark, so thirteen photos were retouched to remove one by cloning the
fabric or leather beside it: the pack lid logos on `basecamp-pack-1.jpg`, `basecamp-pack-2.jpg`
and `tile-packs.jpg`, the pack logos on `daybreak-pack-1.jpg` and `daybreak-pack-2.jpg`, the
embroidered chest marks on `merino-top-w-1.jpg` and `merino-top-w-2.jpg` and the faint one on
`summit-down-m-1.jpg`, the chest label on `ridgeline-shell-m-2.jpg`, the tag, side print and sole
print on `canyon-boot-w-1.jpg`, the tongue tabs on `canyon-boot-w-2.jpg`, the boot logos on
`tile-footwear.jpg` and the shoe logo on `trailhead-pants-w-black-1.jpg`. Other photos are cropped
to leave a mark out: `trailhead-pants-m-*` and `merino-top-m-*` stop below a cap or beanie label,
`alpine-fleece-m-*` and `tile-layers.jpg` stop above a trouser logo, `trail-socks-2.jpg` leaves out
a sticker, `trail-headlamp-*` leaves out the headband's print and `peak-beanie-black-1.jpg` stops
above a jacket logo. People appear as wearers. `hero-trail.jpg` is mirrored left to right, so the
darker side of the sky sits under the headline.

The two photos of a product show the same piece: `ridgeline-shell-w-2`, `trailhead-pants-w-2`,
`alpine-fleece-m-2`, `trailhead-pants-m-2`, `canyon-boot-m-2`, `trekking-poles-2`,
`trail-headlamp-2`, `trail-bottle-2` and `sun-hat-2` are closer crops of the product's first photo,
and the others are two shots of the same piece from the same photographer's shoot. The colour
photos show the same kind of piece in that colour: `merino-top-m-navy-1.jpg` is the same top in
navy from the same shoot, while `trailhead-pants-w-black-1.jpg`, `trailhead-pants-m-black-1.jpg`,
`summit-down-w-black-1.jpg`, `summit-down-m-black-1.jpg`, `trail-bottle-black-1.jpg`,
`peak-beanie-black-1.jpg` and `peak-beanie-oat-1.jpg` are other photographers' pieces in that colour. To replace a photo, save a
new file under the same name.
