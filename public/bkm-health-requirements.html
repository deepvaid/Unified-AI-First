What it takes to run BKM Health on Commerce Cloud

BKM sells 8 supplements, mostly on subscription, to 33 countries from a Shopify store at bkm.health. This page lists what that store needs from a theme and from the platform, and how much of it Commerce Cloud has today.

Commerce Cloud statuses come from the UAT capture in the design-system repo (Sept to Oct 2026), not the live product. The capture holds real section and file names but stand-in contents, so Missing means "not found in the capture". The last section lists what engineering should confirm.

Summary

requirements, by status

    Launch blockers

    Eight things stand between BKM and a launch

    • Subscriptions

      Plans, prepaid supplies, recurring billing and a self-service portal. Nothing exists, and it is BKM's core revenue model.

      P01 to P05, T38

    • Selling across markets

      Per-market currency and prices, market URLs, shipping zones and rates, VAT, and European payment methods.

      P06, P07, P09 to P11, T06

    • Structured product content

      Subtitle, chips, ingredients, supplement facts and FAQs have nowhere to live on a product. Several product-page sections read from it.

      P13

    • A template per product, and sections in code

      Every BKM product page is its own composition; Commerce Cloud has one template per type. Whether a developer can add a coded section to the builder is unconfirmed.

      T11, T12

    • A handful of universal sections

      Video hero, tabbed collections, accordion, comparison table, announcement bar.

      T01, T22, T24, T30, T43, T48

    • Reviews

      Only a rating snippet exists. BKM shows stars on every card and a review list on each product.

      P17, T35

    • Consent, pixels and a place for scripts

      Needed to trade in the EU and to keep BKM's ad attribution working.

      T07, T13, P21, P22

    • Migration

      Live subscriptions and their stored payment methods are the risky part.

      P27

    Already covered: catalog basics, collections, promotions, customer accounts, blog, search, policy pages and most plain content sections. Email, SMS, popups, chat and helpdesk can come from Marketing Cloud and Service Cloud.
