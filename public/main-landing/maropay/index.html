Checkout

Your checkout, with Maropay built in.

Everything you choose shows on your own store — the product page, the cart and checkout.

  • Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal as one-tap express buttons.
  • Cards offered as Maropay, with Visa, Mastercard and Amex.
  • Your method order, your default and your pay button's words.
  • Buy now, pay later, with the instalment shown under the price.

Activation

Switch each store when it's ready.

See exactly what shoppers will see before anything changes.

  1. Link the store. Its checkout stays as it is.
  2. Choose methods. A live preview shows your checkout as you go.
  3. Run a test checkout. A test payment goes through end to end.
  4. Review what changes, then activate. Fees and methods side by side with your current provider.

Nothing changes at checkout until you activate.

Payouts

Every payout, line by line.

Know what's coming, when it lands and what's inside it.

  • Each payout opens into its payments, fees, refunds and disputes — and adds up.
  • The next payout as an estimate, with the date it's due.
  • Every transaction with its method, order and store.
  • Export transactions and payouts to CSV.

Disputes

Respond before the deadline.

When a shopper disputes a payment, you see it next to the order with everything you need to answer.

  • The deadline written out, with the days you have left.
  • An evidence checklist, saved as a draft until you send it.
  • The amount held and the dispute fee, in plain sums.
  • Maropost support first, with the references they need.
