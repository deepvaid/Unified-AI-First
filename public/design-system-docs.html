Principles

Four rules carry the whole system

Tokens are the contract

Never hardcode a color, spacing, radius, or shadow. Every value traces to tokens.json, rebuilt into SCSS, CSS, and TS by npm run tokens:build.

Border over shadow

Cards are flat border rounded="lg". Depth comes from a hairline and a tint, not elevation. Shadows exist — sm/md/lg — and are spent sparingly.

Storybook renders the truth

One stylesheet manifest — app-styles.ts — feeds both the app and Storybook, so a story is proof, not an approximation.

Empty is not error

Nothing to show gets MpEmptyState with a next step. Something failed gets MpErrorState with a recovery path. They never merge.

Do & don't

Do
Cards: flat border rounded="lg". Depth is a hairline + tint.
Don't
Elevation shadows on resting cards — md/lg shadows are reserved for menus and overlays.
Do
Forms open in MpFormDrawer; confirms go through MpConfirmDialog (destructive → danger).
Don't
Raw v-dialog for forms or confirms — the only form-in-dialog is MpMoveToFolderDialog.
Do
Status columns use MpStatusChip with the correct type — the color map is the component's job.
Don't
Hand-pick chip colors per page, or use mdi-* icons in new code.
Do
Every list ships MpEmptyState (nothing to show) and MpErrorState (something failed) as separate states.
Don't
One vague "No data" screen doing both jobs — the user can't tell whether to create or to retry.
