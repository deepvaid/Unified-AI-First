# POS design system

The in-store register — Retail Cloud's POS, used by counter cashiers in Australia and Canada, mostly on
iPads (about 70%) and Android tablets. It has its own fixed visual language, built from the same tokens
pipeline as the rest of Marobase and shown live in `/commerce/:id/retail/pos-preview`.

## The look

- **Light and fixed.** A counter terminal never follows the app theme. The palette is
  `color.light.pos.*`, with no dark twin; stories here stay light under the Theme toggle.
- **Arm's length, one hand.** Type sits a step above the back office's, and nothing tappable is under
  **44px** (`component.pos.touchTarget`). Charge and the cart actions are 52.
- **Ink, not brand.** Near-black ink carries the primary action (Charge, Apply, Done); the retail teal
  marks selection (tender, product, customer). Status lives in four text-safe `*Ink` on `*Soft` pairs.

## Rules

1. **44px floor.** Every button, chip, row and close mark reaches it — visually or with a hit area. The one
   exception is a link inside a sentence (`PosButton variant="link"`, 32px), as WCAG 2.5.8 allows.
2. **Status in words.** Colour and icon reinforce a status; the text always says it.
3. **Text-safe pairs only.** Every ink is ≥ 4.5:1 on the surface, the background and its own soft fill,
   enforced by `npm run contrast:check`. Use `var(--mp-color-light-pos-*)`; never shorten to `--pos-*`
   (that already means *positive*).
4. **Money arrives formatted.** Components take strings ("$54.60", "−$5.00") from the store's locale and
   tax rules (`saleModel.ts`); none of them do arithmetic.
5. **Every sheet is a dialog.** `PosSheet` names it, keeps Tab inside, closes on Escape and gives focus
   back to what opened it.
