# Lumos demo: the walkthrough script

For a sales person showing the Lumos Home (demo) store to a prospect. About 10 minutes live.
There is no film of this store.

Lumos is a made-up furniture and home store, selling in US dollars: sofas, chairs, tables,
storage, beds and bedding, lighting, rugs, decor and a few outdoor pieces from six made-up makers.
Every piece lists its measurements, materials, care, assembly and warranty. It has no
subscriptions and no size charts. Nothing is charged and nothing leaves your browser.

## Before you start

1. Open the store's page: **Sales channels › Lumos Home (demo)**.
2. In its **Settings** tab, press **Restore original store**, then **Restore store**. This puts
   back the theme (its drafts and copies are removed), the products, campaigns, chatbot and search
   rules, and reconnects Merchandise Cloud, so the demo starts the same way every time. Orders you
   placed before stay in Commerce.
3. Press **View store**. Keep the store in one tab and the admin in another.
4. If the wishlist heart in the header shows a number, open it and remove the pieces: the
   wishlist is kept in this browser.
5. If the page was open while a new version went out, reload it once.

## The story in one line

Furniture is bought by the room and by the measurement. The shopper browses rooms and makers,
saves a few pieces, checks a sofa's size and fabric, and buys it with a code. The merchant sees
every part of the page as a section they can move, and swaps the whole store for another theme
and back in a minute.

## More to show if they ask

- **A colour without its own photo:** **Mesa Coffee Table** in **Walnut** shows the oak photos and
  says "Shown in Oak".
- **Sizes that change the price:** the **Weave Wool Rug** is $349 at 5 × 8 ft and $549 at 8 × 10 ft;
  the **Haven Bed Frame** and the **Fold Dining Table** come in two sizes too.
- **Sold out:** **Ergo Task Chair** in **Black** is sold out.
- **A sale price:** **Sale** has the Stone Side Table at $219 instead of $269 and the Ceramic Vase
  Trio at $89 instead of $109. COZY20 takes 20% off the side table too, as its message says: $175.20.
- **Newest first:** press **Browse rooms** in the hero (every piece) and sort by **Newest**: the five new arrivals lead.
- **Search rules:** search **desk lamp** (the store's lights: the pendant, the floor lamp and the
  lanterns), **bookcase** (the bookshelf), **patio** (the outdoor pieces), **delivery** (the delivery page).
- **Pages:** the footer has an FAQ, **Delivery and returns**, a **Care guide**, and **Accessibility**
  beside the privacy policy and terms.
- **Phone:** make the store's tab narrow. The room carousel shows one room at a time, filters open
  as a sheet, and the footer stacks.

## What not to promise

These are not part of this demo. If asked, say so.

- Reviews and ratings on products: the hero's rating is a line of copy.
- Delivery to a room of choice, assembly service, delivery dates or scheduling, and fabric samples.
- A wishlist that follows the shopper to another device, or shared wishlists.
- Room planners, 3D or "see it in your room".
- Click & collect, subscriptions, financing and "Notify me when it's back".
- Search suggestions while typing, and spelling correction.
- Email opens and clicks: a sent campaign reads "Sending".

## If something looks wrong

- **The numbers differ from this script:** someone used the demo before you. Restore the store.
- **The store looks like Vitaheal, Northfold or Torqline:** another theme is applied. Restore the store.
- **No discount code field in the cart:** no campaign with a code is running. Restore the store.
- **No search field in the header:** Merchandise Cloud was disconnected. Restore the store, or
  connect it again in Merchandising.
- **The wishlist already has pieces in it:** they were saved in this browser before. Remove them
  with their hearts.
