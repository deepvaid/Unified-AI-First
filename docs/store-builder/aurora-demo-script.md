# Aurora demo: the walkthrough script

For a sales person showing the Aurora Outdoor (demo) store to a prospect. About 10 minutes live.
There is no film of this store.

Aurora is a made-up outdoor clothing and footwear store for women and men, selling in US dollars:
down jackets, rain shells, fleece, merino base layers, hiking pants and boots, plus a few packs and
accessories. Every piece comes in sizes and colors, with a size chart for clothes, boots, socks and
hats. It has no subscriptions. Nothing is charged and nothing leaves your browser.

The store is the showcase of the Aurora theme, rebuilt from the UAT theme Aurora 1.8.9: the same
header and footer, the full-width hero, the Featured Collections slider, the monochrome colors and
the framed product card. Headings use a real serif, Source Serif 4, where UAT shows the browser's
Times by mistake.

## Before you start

1. Open the store's page: **Sales channels › Aurora Outdoor (demo)**.
2. In its **Settings** tab, press **Restore original store**, then **Restore store**. This puts
   back the theme (its drafts and copies are removed), the products, size charts, campaigns, chatbot
   and search rules, and reconnects Merchandise Cloud, so the demo starts the same way every time.
   Orders you placed before stay in Commerce.
3. Press **View store**. Keep the store in one tab and the admin in another.
4. If the heart in the header shows a number, open it and remove the pieces: the wishlist is kept
   in this browser.
5. If the page was open while a new version went out, reload it once.

## The story in one line

Outdoor clothing is bought by the weather and by the size. The shopper checks how warm a jacket is
and which size to take, buys it with a code, and asks the assistant about boots. The merchant sees
every part of the page as a section they can move, and swaps the whole store for another theme and
back in a minute.

## More to show if they ask

- **The phone:** make the store's tab narrow. The header becomes a menu button, the wordmark and the
  tools, with the search field on a row of its own; the menu opens from the left. The slider shows
  one collection at a time, and the footer's link columns fold away under their heads, each with a **+** to open it.
- **Layering:** ask the chat "How should I layer for a cold hike?", then "for women". It names the
  store's base layer, fleece, down jacket and rain shell, and shows them.
- **Socks and hats:** ask "What size socks for a men's 10?" or "What size sun hat for a 23 inch
  head?": the answer comes from the socks and hats size charts.
- **Sold out and low:** the **Women’s Canyon Hiking Boot** is sold out in 8, the **Peak Beanie** in
  Black, and the **Basecamp 45L Pack** in Spruce says **Only 2 left**.
- **A sale price:** **Best sellers** on the home page and **Sale** have the Insulated Trail Bottle at $32
  instead of $40; **Sale** also has the Packable Sun Hat at $35 instead of $45. TRAIL20 is for jackets only,
  so it doesn't touch them.
- **Newest first:** open **Shop all** and sort by **Newest**: the headlamp, the beanie, the backpacking
  pack and the two fleeces, the season's new arrivals, lead. On **Packs & accessories** they lead already.
- **Search rules:** search **puffer** (the down jackets), **backpack** (both packs), **toque** (the
  beanie), **size chart** (the size guide page).
- **The community page:** the social icons in the header lead to **Join us on the trail**, the store's
  page about its trips and photos.
- **The editor:** on the store's page press **Edit theme**. The header's **Menu style** is
  **Stacked** and the footer's layout **Menus and newsletter**: Aurora's own header and footer.

## What not to promise

These are not part of this demo. If asked, say so.

- Reviews and ratings on products.
- Weather forecasts, or a promise of how warm a shopper will be: the pages give each jacket's rating,
  and the assistant says what it can't promise.
- Social feeds in the store: the social icons lead to the store's own community page.
- Gear rental, repairs, trip bookings and gift cards.
- A wishlist that follows the shopper to another device, or shared wishlists.
- Click & collect, subscriptions, back-in-stock alerts and "Notify me when it's back".
- Search suggestions while typing, and spelling correction.
- Email opens and clicks: a sent campaign reads "Sending".

## If something looks wrong

- **The numbers differ from this script:** someone used the demo before you. Restore the store.
- **The store looks like Vitaheal, Northfold, Torqline or Lumos:** another theme is applied. Restore the store.
- **No bar above the header, or no discount code field in the cart:** no campaign with a code is
  running. Restore the store.
- **No search field in the header:** Merchandise Cloud was disconnected. Restore the store, or
  connect it again in Merchandising.
- **The wishlist already has pieces in it:** they were saved in this browser before. Remove them
  with their hearts.
