# Northfold demo: the walkthrough script

For a sales person showing the Northfold (demo) store to a prospect. About 9 minutes live. There
is no film of this store.

Northfold is a made-up clothing brand for women and men, selling in US dollars. It has no
subscriptions. Nothing is charged and nothing leaves your browser.

## Before you start

1. Open the store's page: **Sales channels › Northfold (demo)**.
2. In its **Settings** tab, press **Restore original store**, then **Restore store**. This puts
   back the theme (its drafts and copies are removed), products, size charts, campaigns, chatbot
   and search rules, and reconnects Merchandise Cloud, so the demo starts the same way every time.
   Orders you placed before stay in Commerce.
3. Press **View store**. Keep the store in one tab and the admin in another.
4. If the page was open while a new version went out, reload it once.

## The story in one line

A clothing brand sells every piece in sizes and colours. The shopper finds the right size, buys
with a code and follows the order; the merchant changes a colour and a size chart, and the store
follows at once.

## Steps

| # | Where | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store, home | Scroll the home page. Open **Women** in the menu. | "The brand's own look: serif headlines, square corners, black buttons. Every section on this page is one the merchant can move or edit." |
| 2 | Store, Women | Under **Size** pick **M**, under **Color** pick **Black**. On a phone, press **Filter and sort** first. Remove the **Size M** chip, then put it back. | "Filters count what is in stock in that size and colour: 5 of 12 styles. The address keeps the filters, so Back works and a link can be shared." |
| 3 | Store, product | Open **Outerwear**, then **Linen Overshirt**. Pick **Sand**. Press **Add to cart** without a size. | "The photos follow the colour. Without a size it asks for one instead of greying out the button." |
| 4 | Store, size guide | Point at **L**: it is struck through. Pick **M**. Press **Size guide**, switch to **cm**, then close it. | "Sold-out sizes stay visible. The size guide is the chart the merchant keeps in the admin, in inches or centimetres, with how to measure." |
| 5 | Store, cart | Press **Add to cart**, then **View cart**. Type `LAYERS20` in **Discount code**, press **Apply**. | "The line says Size M · Sand, with the Sand photo. The code is from the bar at the top: 20% off outerwear, so $98 becomes $78.40." |
| 6 | Store, checkout | Press **Checkout**, then **Pay**. | "Checkout in dollars, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Delivery is free, in 3 to 5 business days. The details are filled in for the demo." |
| 7 | Store, account | On the confirmation press **Track your order**. Press **Your account** in the breadcrumb, then the order. | "The shopper's own order page: what they bought, the discount, the address and where it is. The account lists every order from this store." |
| 8 | Store, quick add | Open **Knitwear**. Press **+** on **Merino Crewneck Sweater**, pick **M**, press **Add to cart**, then **Keep shopping**. | "Quick add from the list: size and colour in one sheet, without leaving the page." |
| 9 | Store, search | Press the search icon. Search **jumper**, then **returns**. | "No product is called a jumper; a synonym finds the three sweaters. 'Returns' goes straight to the refund policy, through a redirect." |
| 10 | Store, chat | Open **Need help?**. Type "What size merino sweater for a 38 inch chest?", then "Can I wash the merino?". | "The assistant reads the piece's own size chart and fit note, and its care label. It never gives health advice." |
| 11 | Admin, Commerce | **Orders**: open the newest order. | "The line says M · Sand with the right photo, and the code and discount are on it." |
| 12 | Admin, Products | Open **Linen Overshirt**. Point at the variant grid. Press **Edit options and variants**, **Continue** twice, change the **Sand** colour, press **Publish**. Back in the store tab, look at the overshirt. | "Fifteen variants, grouped by colour, with stock per size. Change a swatch and the store follows, in the tab that was already open." |
| 13 | Admin, Products › Size charts | Open **Women's tops**. Change **Bust** for **M**, press **Save chart**, then **View in store** and open the size guide. | "One chart serves every women's top. Change it once and six product pages show it." |
| 14 | Admin, store › Campaigns | Show **Fall layers**: its code, orders and revenue. Open its menu, **Announce by email**. | "A campaign is a message on the store, a code, and its results. One click writes the email to this store's customers." |
| 15 | Admin, Merchandising | Open **Merchandising** for the store: **Synonyms**, **Redirects**, **Pinning**. | "The rules behind step 9. The merchant changes them here, and search follows at once." |
| 16 | Admin, theme | On the store's page press **Edit theme**. In **Icon row**, select **Free shipping** and change its title. | "Every section is editable, and the page redraws as you type." |
| 17 | Admin, store › Themes | Open **Themes**. In **Theme library** press **Apply** on **Vitaheal**, read the list, press **Apply theme**. Press **Show store** in the message. Back on **Themes**, press **Restore original store**, then **Restore store**. | "A theme here is a whole store. Apply Vitaheal and this store sells supplements, in kronor, with Vitaheal's two subscription plans; orders stay. Restore puts Northfold back as it started: its look, products, size charts and campaigns." |

## More to show if they ask

- **Only a few left:** Merino Crewneck Sweater in Navy, size M, says "Only 2 left in M".
- **A sale price:** Washed Silk Blouse shows $88.00 and $118.00, 25% off.
- **A sold-out size chosen:** pick **L** on the Linen Overshirt in Sand: "Sorry, L is sold out.
  Choose another size."
- **Buy now:** on a product page, straight to checkout with that size and colour.
- **A fibre to avoid:** ask the chat "I'm allergic to wool". It shows pieces without wool and
  where every fabric is listed, with no medical advice.
- **Sort:** on any collection, **Sort by** price, newest or name.
- **Phone:** make the store's tab narrow. Filters and sort open as a sheet; the menu has search.
- **A product's size guide:** Products › any top › the **Size guide** card picks its chart.

## What not to promise

These are not part of this demo. If asked, say so.

- The editor's **Styles** tab does not change the button, surface or divider colours yet.
- Click & collect, subscriptions, reviews and "Notify me when it's back".
- Hover image swap on product cards, and a sticky buy bar on phones.
- Search filters by fit or brand, suggestions while typing, and spelling correction.
- Email opens and clicks: a sent campaign reads "Sending".

## If something looks wrong

- **The numbers differ from this script:** someone used the demo before you. Restore the store.
- **The store looks like Vitaheal or Torqline:** another theme is applied. Restore the store.
- **No discount code field in the cart:** no campaign with a code is running. Restore the store.
- **No search icon on the store:** Merchandise Cloud was disconnected. Restore the store, or
  connect it again in Merchandising.
