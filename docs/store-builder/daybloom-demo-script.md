# Daybloom demo: the walkthrough script

For a sales person showing the Daybloom (demo) store to a prospect. About 10 minutes live. There
is no film of this store.

Daybloom is a made-up supplements brand in the United States, selling in US dollars. It is the
health-and-supplements store on the look of the Aurora theme: a menu by goal and format, promises
up front, supplement facts on every product, and a "Subscribe and save" plan with a delivery
every 1, 2 or 3 months. Nothing is charged and nothing leaves your browser.

Vitaheal (demo) is the other health store, and has the same menu, promises strip and product
facts. Use Vitaheal for kronor, plans of a fixed supply ("3-month supply") and the film; use
Daybloom for dollars, the Aurora look, bundles and a plan with delivery options.

## Before you start

1. Open the store's page: **Sales channels › Daybloom (demo)**.
2. In its **Settings** tab, press **Restore original store**, then **Restore store**. This puts
   back the theme (its drafts and copies are removed), products, plan, campaigns, chatbot and
   search rules, and reconnects Merchandise Cloud, so the demo starts the same way every time.
   Orders and subscriptions you made before stay in Commerce.
3. Press **View store**. Keep the store in one tab and the admin in another.
4. If the page was open while a new version went out, reload it once.

## The story in one line

A supplements brand sells trust first: what is in the bottle, what it is for, and a subscription
with no lock-in. The shopper finds a product by goal, subscribes in one step and manages it
alone; the merchant sets the plan, ships the order and edits the page without a developer.

## To start from the very beginning (2 minutes)

Use this before step 1 when the prospect asks how a store comes to be. It makes a second store
and leaves Daybloom (demo) as it is.

| # | Where | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Admin, Sales channels | Press **Create sales channel**. Choose **Web Store**, **Continue**. Type a name, **Continue**. Pick the **Daybloom** theme, **Continue**. Read **Starts with**, press **Create sales channel**. | "A new store in four steps. Start blank, or from a theme: a theme brings its look, products, pages, campaigns and plan." |
| B | Admin, the new store | Watch **Setting up …**: the steps finish one by one. | "The store is put together for you: your Commerce Cloud account, the storefront, checkout, and its address. You can leave this page; it carries on." |
| C | Admin, the new store | When it says ready, point at **Launch your store**. Press **Mark as done** on **Check your shipping**. Press **Open tax categories**, then go back. | "Eight steps to launch, in order. The ones the store can check are ticked by themselves: it has products, a theme, a way to pay. The rest you tick when you have looked." |
| D | Admin, the new store | Press **Show store** in the message, or **View store**. | "And it is live, as a copy of the theme. From here it is the store you have just seen." |

Afterwards, delete the store from its **Settings** tab (**Delete store**), so the next demo starts clean.

## More to show if they ask

- **Search:** press the search box. **tired** finds the energy products through a synonym;
  **refund** goes straight to the refund policy. The rules are in **Merchandising** for the store.
- **A bundle:** **Bundles** in the menu. A bundle costs less than its products one by one, is
  sold once only, and its facts say what is in it.
- **The subscription page:** **Subscribe and save** in the menu: what each option saves, how it
  works in three steps, and how to stop.
- **Quality and testing**, **FAQ** and **Accessibility** under **Learn** and in the footer.
- **A failed payment, a paused and a cancelled subscription:** Commerce › Subscriptions ›
  Subscribers has one of each, seeded.
- **Phone:** make the store's tab narrow. The menu opens **Shop** and **Learn** in place.
- **A new plan:** Commerce › Subscriptions › Plans › **New plan** › **Plan with delivery
  options**. A product can be on up to three plans.

## What not to promise

These are not part of this demo. If asked, say so.

- Reviews and star ratings, "Notify me when it's back", and a product quiz.
- Sizes or flavors to pick on a product page: every Daybloom product is sold in one size.
- Quantity discounts ("buy 3, save more") and building your own bundle.
- A cart that slides out, and a free-shipping progress bar: shipping is free on every order.
- Prices per market, other languages, and local payment methods such as Klarna.
- Filters and sort on a collection page of this theme (the Northfold and Torqline themes have them).
- The menu's columns and picture are part of the theme: the store's **Navigation** page lists
  the header's five links, not what is inside the panels.
- Email opens and clicks: a sent campaign reads "Sending".
- Shipping zones and rates, tax rates per region, a logo upload and a custom domain: the launch
  checklist names these steps, and three of them are ticked by hand.

## If something looks wrong

- **The numbers differ from this script:** someone used the demo before you. Restore the store.
- **The store looks like Vitaheal, Northfold or Torqline:** another theme is applied. Restore the store.
- **No discount code field in the cart:** no campaign with a code is running. Restore the store.
- **No search box on the store:** Merchandise Cloud was disconnected. Restore the store, or
  connect it again in Merchandising.
- **No Subscribe and save on a product page:** the plan is paused, or the product is a bundle.
  Commerce › Subscriptions › Plans shows which.
